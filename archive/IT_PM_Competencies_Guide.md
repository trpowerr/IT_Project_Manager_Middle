# Справочник компетенций IT-менеджеров проектов: Middle+ и Senior уровни

*Второе издание. Основано на стандартах PMI, PMBOK 7, PRINCE2, ISO 21500, SAFe и ITIL 4*

---

## СТРУКТУРА ДОКУМЕНТА

Этот справочник организован по модели **PMI PMCD Framework (Project Manager Competency Development Framework)**, которая определяет компетенции через три ключевые измерения:

1. **Performance Competencies** — результаты, которых менеджер достигает в проектной деятельности
2. **Personal Competencies** — внутренние качества, поведение и черты характера
3. **Профессиональные навыки** — специализированные знания и инструменты для IT

Для каждой компетенции указаны уровни владения:
- **Middle+**: Демонстрирует компетентность с минимальным надзором, управляет проектами средней сложности (3-5 лет опыта)
- **Senior**: Показывает экспертизу, управляет сложными проектами и программами, наставляет других (7-10+ лет опыта)

# РАЗДЕЛ 1: PERFORMANCE COMPETENCIES (Компетенции в проектной деятельности)

Основаны на процессных группах PMBOK 7 и уникальных для IT проектов требованиях.

## 1.1 Initiating / Project Charter Development

### Middle+
- Участие в разработке Project Charter совместно со спонсором
- Определение высокоуровневых целей и успеха проекта
- Идентификация primary стейкхолдеров и их ожиданий
- Документирование бизнес-кейса и обоснования проекта
- Понимание SDLC фаз (Planning, Analysis, Design, Development, Testing, Deployment, Maintenance) для IT проектов

### Senior
- Полная собственность за Project Charter и его утверждение на executive уровне
- Стратегическое выравнивание проекта с organisational strategy и portfolio goals
- Управление competing интересами multiple sponsors и board-level stakeholders
- Определение success metrics, KPIs, и value delivery criteria
- Интеграция проекта в enterprise roadmap и strategic initiatives
- Глубокое понимание SDLC выбор оптимального подхода (Waterfall, Agile, Hybrid) для specific IT contexta

## 1.2 Planning / Project Management Plan Development

### Middle+
- Разработка comprehensive Project Management Plan включая Scope, Schedule, Budget, Quality, Communications
- Выбор appropriate методологии (Waterfall, Agile, Hybrid) с обоснованием выбора
- Создание Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) и детализация deliverables на уровне work packages
- Оценка длительности и ресурсов для IT проектов (development, testing, infrastructure)
- Планирование спринтов в Agile/Scrum environment (User Stories, Story Points, Sprint capacity)
- Определение Risk Register и первоначальные mitigation strategies
- Планирование коммуникаций для team и stakeholders

### Senior
- Strategic planning для multi-project programs и portfolio management
- Tailoring PMBOK 7 методологии к specific organizational context и project constraints
- Optimization allocации ресурсов across multiple projects и teams
- Forecast планирование с использованием historical data и predictive analytics
- Scenario planning и contingency planning для complex, uncertain IT initiatives
- Baseline establishment для scope, schedule и budget
- Integration planning для large-scale IT implementations с multiple dependencies
- Cloud migration planning (AWS, Azure, GCP) если applicable

- Driving DevOps practices и CI/CD pipeline efficiency

# РАЗДЕЛ 3: HARD-СКИЛЛС (Ways of Working)

## 3.1 Методологии управления проектами

### PMBOK 7 (Project Management Body of Knowledge, 7th Edition)

#### Middle+
- **Знание 8 Performance Domains**:
  - Stakeholders: Identifying, analyzing, engaging stakeholders effectively
  - Team: Building high-performing teams, psychological safety
  - Development Approach: Selecting Waterfall, Agile, или Hybrid based на project context
  - Planning: Creating plans для scope, schedule, resources, quality, risk
  - Work Performance: Managing execution, quality assurance, continuous improvement
  - Delivery: Ensuring value delivery throughout project
  - Measurement: Defining success metrics, KPIs, tracking performance
  - Uncertainty: Identifying, assessing, responding к risks и opportunities

- **12 Guiding Principles** (базовый уровень):
  - Stewardship, Value Delivery, Adaptability, Stakeholder Engagement, Complexity, Quality, Collaboration, Tailoring, Systems Thinking, Organizational Strategy Alignment

#### Senior
- **Mastery всех 8 Performance Domains** с ability адаптировать under different contexts
- **Deep knowledge 12 Principles** — applying intuitively в complex situations
- **Tailoring expertise** — customizing PMBOK подход для specific industry, organization type, project complexity
- **Integration с organizational processes** — embedding PMBOK into enterprise PMO standards
- **PMBOK 8 emerging trends** (если applicable в организации)

### Agile / Scrum Framework

#### Middle+
- **Scrum основы** (знание всех 5 events, 3 roles, 3 artifacts):
  - Roles: Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team
  - Events: Sprint Planning, Daily Standup, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective
  - Artifacts: Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment
- **User Story creation**: Writing effective user stories с acceptance criteria
- **Sprint planning**: Capacity planning, story estimation (story points, Fibonacci)
- **Backlog management**: Grooming, prioritization, refinement
- **Velocity tracking**: Measuring team capacity, forecasting completion dates
- **Retrospectives**: Facilitating learning, identifying continuous improvements

#### Senior
- **Scaled Agile (SAFe) understanding** (if enterprise context):
  - Portfolio Management: Strategic theme management, investment funding
  - Program Execution: Program Increments (PI), release management
  - Team Level: Advanced Scrum practices, XP techniques
  - Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)
  
- **LeSS Framework** (Large-Scale Scrum for larger organizations):
  - Single Product Backlog architecture
  - Cross-team coordination mechanisms
  - Area Product Owners (for LeSS Huge)
  - Scaling without bureaucracy
  
- **Kanban / Lean Principles**:
  - WIP limits, flow optimization
  - Continuous delivery vs. fixed iterations
  - Metrics: Lead time, cycle time, throughput
  
- **Hybrid approaches**: Combining Waterfall + Agile для different project phases

### PRINCE2 Framework (Project in Controlled Environments)

#### Middle+
- **7 PRINCE2 Principles** (базовое понимание):
  - Continued business justification, Learn from experience, Defined roles, Manage by stages, Manage by exception, Focus на products, Tailor to context
  
- **Processes**:
  - Initiating a Project (IP) — starting phase
  - Directing a Project (DP) — governance, steering committee interface
  - Controlling a Stage (CS) — managing stage-level activities
  - Managing Product Delivery (MPD) — quality assurance
  - Closing a Project (CP) — final deliverables acceptance
  
- **Themes**:
  - Business Case, Organization, Quality, Plans, Risk, Change, Progress

#### Senior
- **Full PRINCE2 Practitioner** level knowledge (if working in PRINCE2 environment)
- **PRINCE2 Agile**: Combining PRINCE2 governance с Agile flexibility
- **Portfolio governance**: PRINCE2 в context of program/portfolio management
- **Regulatory environments**: PRINCE2 для regulated industries (Finance, Healthcare)

### ISO 21500 (Guidance on Project Management)

#### Middle+
- **Базовое знание**: ISO 21500 як international standard для PM
- **10 Subject Groups** (vs. PMBOK Knowledge Areas):
  - Integration, Stakeholders, Scope, Resources, Time, Cost, Risk, Quality, Procurement, Communications

#### Senior
- **Integration с другими стандартами**: ISO 21500, PMBOK, PRINCE2, IPMA
- **Organizational Standard Development**: Creating tailored PM framework combining multiple standards
- **Competency Assessment**: Using ISO 21500 competency definitions для team development

## 3.2 IT-специфичные методологии и фреймворки

#### Middle+
- **Фазы SDLC**:
  - Planning & Requirement Analysis: Feasibility studies, resource allocation
  - Defining Requirements (SRS): Software Requirements Specification documentation
  - Design Architecture: HLD (High-Level Design), LLD (Low-Level Design)
  - Development (Coding): Code reviews, unit testing, static analysis
  - Testing: QA, UAT, security testing, performance testing
  - Deployment: Release planning, rollout coordination
  - Maintenance & Support: Post-launch support, hotfixes
  
- **SDLC Models**:
  - Waterfall: Sequential,適для stable requirements
  - V-Model: Test-driven approach
  - Iterative: Incremental delivery
  - Agile/Scrum: Incremental with sprint-based delivery
  
- **Role of PM**: Coordinating across all SDLC phases, managing dependencies

#### Senior
- **SDLC Optimization**: Tailoring SDLC для specific technology stacks, team capabilities
- **Enterprise SDLC Standards**: Developing organization-wide SDLC frameworks
- **Architecture Review**: Understanding system architecture, API design, database design decisions
- **DevOps Integration**: SDLC integration с CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code

#### Middle+
- **CI/CD Basics**: Understanding continuous integration и continuous deployment benefits

  - Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD: Build automation, test execution

#### Senior

- **Monitoring & Observability**: APM tools, logging, alerting for production systems
- **Security in DevOps**: DevSecOps practices, vulnerability scanning, compliance automation
- **Scaling CI/CD**: Managing pipelines for large organizations, distributed teams

### Cloud Architecture and Migration

#### Middle+
- **Cloud Fundamentals**:
  - IaaS vs. PaaS vs. SaaS models
  - Public, Private, Hybrid cloud models
  - Multi-cloud strategies
  
- **Cloud Platforms Awareness**:
  - AWS: EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda (awareness level)

- **Compliance Auditing**: Regular GDPR audits, breach response planning
- **International Data Transfers**: Mechanisms for transferring data outside EU (SCCs, adequacy decisions)

### ISO 27001 (Information Security Management)

#### Middle+
- **Базовое понимание**:
  - ISMS (Information Security Management System): Framework для managing information security
  - 14 domains of controls: Governance, Asset management, Personnel security, Access control, Cryptography, Physical security, Operations, Communications, System acquisition, Supplier relationships, Information security incidents, Business continuity, Compliance

## 8.1 DACH Region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) Specifics

Для пользователя во Франкфурте, важно знать региональные стандарты и предпочтения:

### Стандарты в DACH:
- **PRINCE2** очень популярен в Германии (более чем PMI в некоторых индустриях)

- **IPMA (International Project Management Association)** имеет сильное presence, особенно в Германии
  - IPMA Level C (Senior Project Manager): Эквивалент PMP, но assessment-based
  - IPMA Level B: Program management level
  
### Требования рынка труда:
- Fluency в английском языке (C1) critical для international roles
- PRINCE2 Practitioner часто требуется в больших немецких компаниях
- SAFe popularity растет в enterprise environments
- Compliance требования (GDPR) высокие — experience важна

### Рекомендация:
- Prioritize PMP (international recognition)
- Добавить PRINCE2 Practitioner (европейское преимущество)
- Если работаете в regulated industry — ITIL 4 Managing Professional

# ЗАКЛЮЧЕНИЕ

Этот справочник представляет собой comprehensive guide к компетенциям, необходимым для успешной карьеры в IT Project Management на уровнях Middle+ и Senior. Ключевые takeaways:

1. **Middle+** фокусируется на **excellence в project execution** — успешное управление проектами средней сложности, strong technical execution, effective team leadership.

2. **Senior** фокусируется на **strategic impact** — program management, business value realization, organizational transformation, executive influence.

3. **Современная PM** требует **balance между traditional (PMBOK, PRINCE2) и Agile (Scrum, SAFe, LeSS)** подходами.

4. **IT-специфичные компетенции** (SDLC, Cloud, DevOps, Security, Compliance) становятся все более важны.

5. **AI и автоматизация** теперь part of PM toolkit — это не будущее, это present.

6. **Soft skills и Power Skills** часто differentiator между "хорошим" PM и "отличным" PM.

7. **Continuous learning** — обязательная привычка для carving out successful PM career.

Используйте этот справочник как:
- **Self-assessment tool** — оцените, где вы сейчас
- **Development roadmap** — определите, что нужно выучить далее
- **Hiring guide** — если вы нанимаете PMs
- **Interview preparation** — если вы interviewing на PM роль
