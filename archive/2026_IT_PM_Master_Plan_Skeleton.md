# PART 3: 12-MONTH CURRICULUM SKELETON

## Q1: OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE & BUSINESS ALIGNMENT

### 📅 MONTH 1: Foundation – Roles, Strategy & Financial Basics
*Integration: Yandex Module 1 (Roles/Ops) + Senior Dev Module 1 (Finance/Strategy)*

**Learning Goals:**
1. Audit current processes using Cynefin framework.
2. Master basic P&L (ROI, EBITDA, Margin) for projects.
3. Define strict "Roles & Responsibilities" (RACI) for the current team.

**Weekly Structure:**
*   **Week 1 (Ops):** Team & Processes Audit (Yandex Base). *Output: Process Maturity Map.*
*   **Week 2 (Biz):** Finance for PMs 101. *Output: Calculation of ROI for current project.*
*   **Week 3 (AI):** AI for Administrative Excellence. *Task: Automate meeting minutes & follow-ups.*
*   **Week 4 (Review):** Case Study "The Unprofitable Feature".

**🔗 Integration Focus:**
*   Use Yandex materials to fix *current* operational leaks.
*   Use Senior materials to start speaking the "Language of Business" (Money).

**🤖 AI Copilot Task:**
"Use AI to analyze the last 5 project retrospective reports and identify the top 3 systemic bottlenecks."

---

### 📅 MONTH 2: Stakeholder & Product Value Management
*Integration: Yandex Module 2 (Product/Client) + Senior Dev Module 2 (Stakeholders)*

**Learning Goals:**
1. Advanced Stakeholder Mapping (Power/Interest + Attitude).
2. Requirements Elicitation & CJM (Customer Journey Mapping).
3. Managing "Difficult Conversations" and expectations.

**Weekly Structure:**
*   **Week 1 (Stakeholders):** Political Mapping of the organization. *Output: Influence Map.*
*   **Week 2 (Product):** Value Proposition & CJM. *Output: Value Roadmap.*
*   **Week 3 (Soft Skills):** Conflict Negotiation Lab. *Format: Simulation.*
*   **Week 4 (AI):** AI for Requirements. *Task: Generate User Stories from raw meeting transcripts.*

**🔗 Integration Focus:**
*   Yandex: How to gather requirements (Tactics).
*   Senior: How to manage the *politics* around requirements (Strategy).

---

### 📅 MONTH 3: Advanced Delivery & Program Basics
*Integration: Yandex Module 3 (Process) + Senior Dev Module 3 (Program Mgmt)*

**Learning Goals:**
1. Transition from "Project" (Deliverable) to "Program" (Benefits) thinking.
2. Advanced Scheduling (PERT, Critical Chain).
3. Dependency Management across multiple teams.

**Weekly Structure:**
*   **Week 1 (Delivery):** Flow Metrics (Cycle Time, WIP Limits).
*   **Week 2 (Program):** Benefits Realization Management basics.
*   **Week 3 (Governance):** Setting up Program Governance (Escalation paths).
*   **Week 4 (Review):** Simulation "The Delayed Release".

**🤖 AI Copilot Task:**
"Feed the current project schedule (Gantt/List) to AI and ask it to identify 'Optimism Bias' risks and suggest a 20% buffer allocation."

## Q2: STRATEGIC PORTFOLIO & RISK (SKELETON)

### 📅 MONTH 4: Portfolio Management Fundamentals
*Focus: Prioritization & Strategy Alignment*
*   **Key Topics:** RICE/WSJF prioritization, Portfolio Kanban, Resource Capacity Planning.
*   **AI Task:** AI-driven resource leveling and capacity forecasting.
*   **Key Artifact:** Corporate Portfolio Dashboard (Draft).

### 📅 MONTH 5: Advanced Risk Management (Quant & Qual)
*Focus: Uncertainty & Anti-Fragility*
*   **Key Topics:** Monte Carlo Simulation (Basics), Risk Registers vs. Issues, Mitigation Strategies (Avoid/Transfer/Mitigate).
*   **Integration:** Yandex Risk Matrix + PMBOK 7th Ed Risk Standards.
*   **AI Task:** "Red Teaming" — Ask AI to act as a hacker/competitor and find holes in your project plan.

### 📅 MONTH 6: Financial Governance & Budgeting
*Focus: Money Management at Scale*
*   **Key Topics:** CAPEX vs OPEX, TCO (Total Cost of Ownership), Budget Forecasting, EVM (Earned Value Management) deep dive.
*   **Key Artifact:** Investment Committee Pitch Deck.
